OvertheWeekend

Over the Weekend is an open branding contest run by Koto.

On Friday at 7pm, Koto publishes a short brief describing a fictional brand that needs a brand identity. Anyone can enter. Students, junior designers, self-taught designers, people with day jobs in other fields.

Entries close on Sunday at 7pm. That gives each person 48 hours to read, think, sketch, build and submit.

Why a weekend? Because that is the time most people actually have. And 48 hours is too short to overthink. You make a decision, you commit to it, and you learn from that.
